Everything in one browser

The tools people usually assemble from extensions and extra apps, working together out of the box.

Shields

Blocks ads, trackers, phishing and malware sites. Strips tracking links, upgrades to HTTPS, rejects cookie banners and protects against fingerprinting. Optional Google Safe Browsing with your own key.

Tor windows

Routes a window through Tor with bridges, one-click new identity, Tor-style letterboxing and Safer / Safest levels (WebGL or JavaScript off).

Honest by design

Privacy tools are only worth trusting when they're clear about their limits.

What Nova does

  • No accounts, ads or analytics.
  • Blocks trackers and reduces what sites learn about your device.
  • Hides your IP through Tor or your own VPN.
  • Signed updates, a sandboxed engine and a built-in security checkup.

What no browser can promise

  • Anonymity if you log in or reuse identifiers.
  • Erasing data kept by websites, networks or Windows.
  • Tor Browser's full anonymity set — for high-risk needs use Tor Browser or Tails.
  • Stopping every unknown threat or zero-day.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

Is Nova free?

Yes. No account, subscription or ad network.

Does Nova track me?

No analytics or telemetry. Nova only connects out for the pages you visit and for features you use: signed update checks, filter lists, and optional services you switch on (Safe Browsing, VirusTotal, translation, AI).

Is it Tor Browser?

No. Nova's Tor window routes traffic through Tor and adds letterboxing and safety levels, but it can't match Tor Browser's anonymity set.

Does Nova include a VPN service?

No. It connects to the provider you choose (HTTPS/SOCKS proxy or WireGuard).

Can I use Chrome extensions?

Partly. Extensions that work through content scripts and styles run in Nova. Ones that need a background page, popup or toolbar button don't, and the Chrome Web Store isn't supported.

Will Netflix and other DRM video work?

Maybe. Nova can reuse Widevine from an installed Chrome or Edge, but that isn't guaranteed, and some H.264 video may not play.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10/11 is the main target. macOS and Linux builds are experimental and may be missing Windows-only features such as Defender scanning.
